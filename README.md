# Section B – Survey Design

## Research Topic

**AI-Generated Video and Audio: Quality, Efficiency, and Ethical Considerations in Digital Content Creation**

---

## 1. Objectives of the Survey

The primary objective of this survey is to investigate people's awareness, experience, perceptions, and concerns regarding AI-generated video and audio.

The survey aims to achieve the following objectives:

1. To examine participants' awareness and familiarity with AI-generated video and audio.
2. To investigate participants' exposure to and experience with AI-generated video and audio tools.
3. To understand participants' perceptions of the quality and usefulness of AI-generated content.
4. To examine whether participants believe AI-generated video and audio can improve the efficiency of digital content creation.
5. To identify participants' concerns regarding ethical issues, including misinformation, deepfakes, copyright, and misuse of AI-generated content.
6. To understand participants' views about the future impact of AI-generated video and audio on digital content creation.

The survey results will provide quantitative and qualitative information that can support the group's research into the benefits, limitations, and ethical challenges associated with AI-generated video and audio.

---

## 2. How the Survey Will Be Conducted and Distributed

The survey will be designed as an **online questionnaire** using a platform such as Google Forms or Microsoft Forms.

An online survey is appropriate because it allows participants to access the questionnaire easily using a computer, tablet, or mobile device. It also makes the responses easier to organise and analyse.

The questionnaire will mainly use closed-ended questions, including multiple-choice questions and Likert-scale questions. One open-ended question will be included to allow participants to provide additional comments or opinions.

The survey will be designed to take approximately **3–5 minutes** to complete.

The survey may be distributed through:

* University student communication channels
* Email
* Student group chats
* Social media and online communities

As this research is currently at the **research proposal stage**, the survey will only be designed and will **not be distributed to participants or used to collect actual data** at this stage.

---

## 3. Selected Sample for the Survey

The proposed survey will target approximately **30–50 participants** if the survey is conducted in a later stage of the research project.

The target participants will mainly consist of **university students and young adults who regularly use digital media and online platforms**.

Participants do not need to have technical knowledge of artificial intelligence because the survey is intended to investigate general awareness, experiences, perceptions, and concerns about AI-generated video and audio.

The proposed sampling method is **convenience sampling**, as participants can be recruited through accessible university communication channels and online communities.

The sample will aim to include participants with different levels of familiarity and experience with AI-generated content. This can provide a broader range of opinions about the benefits and potential risks of the technology.

---

The survey will contain **11 questions**, consisting of **10 closed-ended quantitative questions** and **1 open-ended qualitative question**.

The questions are designed to directly support the research objectives.

## 5. Ethical Considerations

Several ethical considerations will be considered when designing and potentially conducting the survey.

### Voluntary Participation

Participation in the survey will be voluntary. Participants will not be forced or pressured to participate and should be able to withdraw from the survey without negative consequences.

### Informed Participation

Participants will be informed about the purpose of the research and the general nature of the survey before completing the questionnaire. The information provided should allow participants to make an informed decision about whether to participate.

### Privacy and Anonymity

The survey will avoid collecting unnecessary personally identifiable information. Participants will not be required to provide information such as their full name, student ID, home address, or other unnecessary personal details.

### Confidentiality

Any information collected from participants will be treated confidentially and used only for the purposes of the research project. Individual responses should not be disclosed in a way that identifies individual participants.

### Neutrality and Avoiding Bias

Survey questions will be written clearly and objectively. Leading or emotionally biased wording will be avoided so that participants can provide their opinions freely.

### Data Security

If the survey is conducted, collected responses will be stored securely and accessed only by authorised members of the research group. Data will not be shared with unrelated third parties.

### Ethical Use of Results

The results will be reported accurately and objectively. The research group will not intentionally manipulate or misrepresent participants' responses to support a particular conclusion.
