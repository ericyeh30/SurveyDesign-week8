# Section B – Survey Design

## 1. Objectives of the Survey

The primary objective of this survey is to investigate people's awareness, experiences, and attitudes towards AI-generated video and audio content.

The survey aims to:

* Identify participants' level of familiarity with AI-generated video and audio.
* Understand how frequently participants use or encounter AI-generated video and audio content.
* Examine participants' perceptions of the quality, usefulness, and efficiency of AI-generated content.
* Investigate participants' concerns about ethical issues, such as misinformation, privacy, copyright, and the potential misuse of AI-generated media.
* Understand whether participants believe AI-generated video and audio will have a significant impact on digital content creation in the future.

The information collected will help the research group understand both the potential benefits and challenges of AI-generated video and audio technology.

---

## 2. How the Survey Will Be Conducted and Distributed

The survey will be conducted as an **online questionnaire** using a platform such as Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. An online survey is suitable because it allows participants to complete the questionnaire conveniently using a computer or mobile device.

The survey will consist mainly of multiple-choice and rating-scale questions, together with one open-ended question. The survey should take approximately **3–5 minutes** to complete.

The survey will be distributed through:

* University student communication channels.
* Email invitations.
* Student group chats and online communities.
* Social media platforms where appropriate.

Participation will be voluntary, and participants will be informed about the purpose of the research before completing the survey.

---

## 3. Selected Sample for the Survey

The expected sample size is approximately **30–50 participants**.

The target participants will mainly be **university students and young adults who use digital media and online platforms**. Participants do not need to have technical knowledge of artificial intelligence because the survey is intended to measure general awareness and perceptions of AI-generated video and audio.

The sample will aim to include participants with different levels of familiarity with AI technology. This will allow the research group to compare perceptions between participants who frequently use AI-generated content and those who have limited experience with it.

A convenience sampling approach will be used because participants will be recruited through accessible university and online communication channels.

---

## 4. Structure of the Survey

The survey will contain at least eight questions, including seven close-ended quantitative questions and one open-ended qualitative question.

### Question 1 – Familiarity with AI-Generated Video and Audio

**How familiar are you with AI-generated video and audio?**

* Very unfamiliar
* Somewhat unfamiliar
* Neutral
* Somewhat familiar
* Very familiar

**Type:** Close-ended / Quantitative

---

### Question 2 – Frequency of Exposure

**How often do you encounter AI-generated video or audio content online?**

* Never
* Rarely
* Sometimes
* Often
* Very often

**Type:** Close-ended / Quantitative

---

### Question 3 – Experience Using AI Tools

**Have you ever used an AI tool to generate or edit video or audio content?**

* Yes, frequently
* Yes, occasionally
* Yes, once or twice
* No, but I am interested in trying it
* No, and I am not interested in trying it

**Type:** Close-ended / Quantitative

---

### Question 4 – Perceived Quality

**How would you rate the overall quality of AI-generated video and audio content that you have seen or used?**

* Very poor
* Poor
* Average
* Good
* Excellent

**Type:** Close-ended / Quantitative

---

### Question 5 – Perceived Efficiency

**To what extent do you agree that AI-generated video and audio can make digital content creation faster and more efficient?**

* Strongly disagree
* Disagree
* Neutral
* Agree
* Strongly agree

**Type:** Close-ended / Quantitative

---

### Question 6 – Ethical Concerns

**How concerned are you about the ethical risks of AI-generated video and audio, such as misinformation, deepfakes, copyright issues, and misuse?**

* Not concerned at all
* Slightly concerned
* Moderately concerned
* Very concerned
* Extremely concerned

**Type:** Close-ended / Quantitative

---

### Question 7 – Future Impact

**How significant do you think AI-generated video and audio will be in the future of digital content creation?**

* Not significant
* Slightly significant
* Moderately significant
* Very significant
* Extremely significant

**Type:** Close-ended / Quantitative

---

### Question 8 – Open-Ended Feedback

**What do you think is the biggest benefit or concern regarding the use of AI-generated video and audio?**

Participants can provide their own response.

**Type:** Open-ended / Qualitative

---

## 5. Ethical Considerations

Several ethical considerations will be taken into account when conducting and distributing the survey.

First, participation will be **voluntary**. Participants will be informed about the purpose of the research and can choose whether or not to participate. They should also be able to stop completing the survey at any time without any negative consequences.

Second, the survey should avoid collecting unnecessary **personal or sensitive information**. Participants will not be required to provide information such as their full name, student ID, address, or other personally identifiable information.

Third, participants' responses will be treated as **confidential and anonymous** where possible. The collected data will only be used for the purposes of the research project and will not be shared for unrelated purposes.

Fourth, participants will be provided with enough information to understand what they are being asked to participate in before completing the survey. The questions will be written clearly and neutrally to avoid misleading participants or influencing their responses.

Finally, the collected survey data will be stored securely and accessed only by authorised members of the research group. When reporting the results, individual participants will not be identifiable.

Overall, the survey will be designed to respect participants' privacy, voluntary participation, informed consent, and confidentiality while collecting useful information for the research project.
